Why this tool

This started from simple handwritten notes—a destination, a hotel, and a rough plan for each part of the day. This app turns that personal habit into a smooth, guided flow. Everything you type is saved automatically in your browser, so you can close the tab, come back later, and pick up right where you left off.

Good to know

All costs are estimates. The total cost is calculated from the air ticket, hotel, and daily trip costs you enter.

How the total cost is calculated

  • Air ticketDestination price, labelled Round-trip
  • HotelHotel price (Double room daily) × number of nights
  • Daily trip costSum of every Morning, Afternoon and Night price you enter
  • TotalAir ticket + Hotel + Daily trip cost

Assumptions

  • The air ticket is for one traveller.
  • The hotel price is for one double room per night.
  • Nights = days − 1. A 3-day trip is 2 hotel nights; a 1-day trip has no hotel cost.
  • All prices are in Hong Kong dollars (HKD).
  • Destination and hotel prices are sample figures and can be edited in js/data.js.

Tips

  • Pick one destination and one hotel — choosing another replaces your current pick. Click a photo to see its details.
  • Dates use dd/mm/yyyy. The start date must be today or later, and the end date can't be before the start date.
  • Everything you enter (dates and every day's plan) is saved automatically in this browser only, so you can move between pages freely. Moving between pages with the menu or the Prev / Next buttons keeps everything you entered. Pressing Plan New Trip (or opening this Home page directly) clears it and starts a fresh plan. Using another browser or device also starts you fresh.
  • On the Journey page, tap the suggested words (Meal, Shopping, Travelling…) to fill in Text quickly, or type your own.
  • There is no save button: your plan is saved as you type on the Journey page, and the Summary page always shows it, with the total cost.
  • On the Summary page, press the Print button and choose Save as PDF to keep a copy of your summary.
